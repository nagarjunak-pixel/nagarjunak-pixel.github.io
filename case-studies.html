Case Study 01 · John Deere (2023–2026)

StoneAge: Multi-Model Agentic Writing Copilot Inside the Engineering System of Record

Part of 750K+ FY26 Automated Requests Multi-Model Routing (GPT-5, GPT-4o, Gemini) Safety-Critical Wording Guardrails Explicit Human-Approved Record Writes

Problem: Technical writers authoring and maintaining complex heavy-machinery service manuals needed AI assistance directly inside the company's engineering system of record (PLM environment), where hallucinated torque specs, unapproved terminology, or missing safety warnings are unacceptable.

Architecture & Solution: Architected StoneAge as a multi-agent LangGraph copilot integrated with React, FastAPI, OpenSearch hybrid lexical+vector retrieval, and Okta SSO:

  • Task-Specific Multi-Model Router: Dynamically routed drafting, structural synthesis, and verification steps across GPT-5, GPT-4o, and Gemini based on reasoning complexity, context window, and latency budget.
  • Search-Plus-Rules Over Fine-Tuning: Deliberately avoided model weight fine-tuning so rapidly evolving product specifications remain 100% auditable and immediately updatable via approved OpenSearch indices and deterministic rule engines.
  • Safety & Auditability: Flagged safety-critical wording automatically; enforced a hard architectural invariant where agents can mutate official engineering records only after a fresh, non-cached human approval, logging every attempt.
Case Study 02 · John Deere · GenAI Rescue

Rescuing a Failing ~30%-Accurate GenAI Product to ~70% Production Accuracy

~30% → ~70% Verified Accuracy (+40 pts) 46 Daily Production Users GPT-4o Vision Diagram Parsing

Problem: An existing single-prompt, single-model GenAI application failed on dense engineering schematics and product-family variations, stalling at roughly ~30% accuracy and losing user trust.

Architecture & Solution: Decomposed the monolithic call into a deterministic step-by-step agentic workflow:

  • Stage 1 — Multimodal Diagram Ingestion: Extracted callouts, part topology, and spatial relationships from engineering diagrams using GPT-4o Vision.
  • Stage 2 — Product-Family Routing: Classified equipment family and routed context strictly to family-specific domain rules and terminology stores, eliminating cross-model contamination.
  • Stage 3 — Rule-Engine Verification Gate: Checked every structured output against deterministic domain constraints before surfacing to authors — lifting production accuracy to ~70% and driving active adoption across 46 specialists.
Case Study 03 · John Deere · Evaluation, GraphRAG & Scale Automation

AIFlow (255 Quality Gates), 6-Agent Neo4j GraphRAG & High-Volume Support Automation

255 Automated Eval Checks ~171K Broken Links Repaired (~8,600 hrs) ~110K Compliant Docs (100% Large Tractor Adoption) ~25% Support Tickets Automated (~83% Accuracy · ~4,700 hrs)

Connected Knowledge (6-Agent Neo4j GraphRAG): Parsed hierarchical engineering manuals without flattening structural hierarchy and built a 6-agent authoring team backed by a Neo4j knowledge graph so every synthesized technical response carries traceable paragraph- and node-level citations.

Measurable Quality (AIFlow): Built AIFlow, an internal library of 255 quality, faithfulness, safety, and formatting checks integrated directly into CI/CD pipelines to block deployments whenever answer quality regresses — paired with asynchronous production telemetry tracking accuracy, drift, cost, and latency with zero user-facing latency penalty.

High-Volume Production Automation:

  • Automatically repaired ~171,000 broken cross-document engineering links (~8,600 hours saved — equivalent to ~3 FTEs per year).
  • Generated ~110,000 standards-compliant documents, achieving 100% adoption across the Large Tractor business unit.
  • Automated ~25% of incoming support tickets end-to-end at ~83% accuracy, routing uncertain confidence bands cleanly to human specialists (~4,700 hours saved).
