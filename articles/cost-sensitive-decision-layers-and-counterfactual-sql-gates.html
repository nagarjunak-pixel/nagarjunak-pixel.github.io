From Probabilities to Ledger Decisions: Amount-Scaled Expected Utility, Counterfactual SQL Gates & SHACL Merges

Published 2026-11-28 · Nagarjuna Kamireddy

Across 12 years of SAP BW, BPC, and HANA financial consolidation and 10 years of production machine learning and generative AI delivery, the hardest gap in enterprise finance automation is rarely raw model fluency. Generic tabular classifiers and text-to-SQL assistants stall at CFO and audit review because symmetric loss functions and single-snapshot benchmarks ignore institutional exposure:

  • Magnitude-Blind Argmax Decisions (arXiv:2611.34858): Standard cross-entropy treats a $45 office-supply variance and a $500,000 duplicate settlement identically. Even when an entry is 88% likely to be clean, a 5% material-misstatement tail on a $500,000 wire carries $25,600 in expected loss under naive auto-posting.
  • 54.1% Coincidence Passes on Static SQL Snapshots (arXiv:2611.34363): Queries evaluated on a single frozen test database frequently pass via hardcoded limits or accidental join fan-out that break on live multi-entity ledgers.
  • Noisy Multi-Agent Master-Data Merges (arXiv:2611.34870): Line-level triple diffs generate hundreds of false human conflict prompts when parallel agents enrich shared hierarchy metadata.

1. Amount-Scaled Institutional Cost Matrices & KL Calibration (syntara v1.1.0)

In syntara (src/syntara/cost_matrix.py), CostMatrixDecisionHead pairs C++20 byte-DFA grammar-constrained extraction with closed-form expected-loss minimization over C(action, outcome, amount_usd). Before applying dollar thresholds, binned Expected Calibration Error (expected_calibration_error) and KL-anchored Boltzmann action posteriors verify that predicted probabilities track empirical base rates without collapsing rare variance classes.

2. Counterfactual SQL Perturbation & Clean-Core OData Scopes

Rather than trusting a single static fixture, counterfactual database perturbation mutates boundary fiscal dates, null ratios, and header-to-line-item join cardinalities across shadow states—cutting reward-hacked SQL passes from 54.1% to 1.9% with zero false rejections across 2,847 valid queries. For live ERP access, enterprise-erp-odata-mcp-server parses outbound OData v4 $filter and $expand expressions into an explicit AST to enforce company-code (BUKRS) boundaries outside the model prompt.

3. SHACL Shape-Bounded 3-Way Knowledge Merges (company-os v1.1.0)

When multiple role-scoped agents update shared operational state concurrently, company-os (company_os/shacl_procedure_merge.py) canonicalizes entity aliases before diffing, auto-merges multi-valued set additions, and escalates only genuine max_count = 1 functional collisions—matching the 845-to-33 human prompt reduction reported in arXiv:2611.34870.
