Prompts Propose, State Machines Govern: Checkpointed Multi-Agent Pipelines, BKT Mastery DAGs & MaaS Assurance

Published 2026-11-27 · Nagarjuna Kamireddy

Across multi-step enterprise agent deployments, leaving state transitions implicit inside system prompts creates two predictable failure modes: tutoring and copilot agents leak premature answers or skip prerequisites, and tool-calling workflows leave corrupted partial writes behind when an upstream call fails mid-run. Across 5,760 failed trajectory continuations in SecondChance (arXiv:2611.31586), unaided frontier models recovered only 16% of the time and left 47.6% of databases in dirty residual states.

1. Checkpointed Editorial StateGraphs & Transactional Rollbacks (langgraph-newsroom v1.1.0)

langgraph-newsroom compiles 3-stage editorial production into an explicit LangGraph StateGraph backed by SqliteSaver checkpoint persistence and interrupt_before=["editorial_gate"] human approval gates. Version v1.1.0 adds TransactionalStageGuard, capturing canonical SHA-256 pre-stage state snapshots and executing deterministic rollbacks whenever an interrupted write or ungrounded numeric claim is detected.

2. Prerequisite DAGs, Bayesian Mastery & HTN Learner Simulation (adaptive-ai-tutor v1.1.0)

adaptive-ai-tutor coordinates 12 specialized GPT-4o agents on Next.js, PostgreSQL, and Prisma. Progression across concepts is governed in code: Kahn topological sorting enforces prerequisite DAG order, Corbett & Anderson Bayesian Knowledge Tracing updates posterior mastery P(L_t), SM-2 schedules retention intervals, and runHtnStudentSimulation (inspired by ReUST, arXiv:2611.34044) verifies zero Socratic answer leaks across 25 multi-turn episodes.

3. Gateway Goodput Pooling & 5-Layer MaaS Assurance (prism-gate, aegis-runtime, calibra)

Because GPU and server CapEx account for 87.3% of per-token serving TCO while tail SLOs cap isolated single-tenant cluster goodput at 59.6% (arXiv:2611.31654), pooling workloads behind prism-gate TCP-Vegas concurrency control and per-hop model cascades recovers stranded capacity while cutting multi-agent DAG spend. Pairing aegis-runtime capability policies with calibra 2PL IRT canary anchors closes the service-integrity and monitor-evasion gaps documented in arXiv:2611.34623 and arXiv:2611.34934.
