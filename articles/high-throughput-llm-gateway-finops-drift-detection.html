Architecting Multi-Tenant Enterprise LLM Gateways: Adaptive Concurrency, Cascade Routing & Statistical Drift Detection

Published November 16, 2026 · AWS Solutions Architect Professional · Open-Source Author of prism-gate

Once an enterprise scales beyond five GenAI applications across multiple business units, direct point-to-point SDK calls to foundation model APIs create three operational failures: noisy-neighbor rate-limit exhaustion, runaway FinOps spend on frontier models for trivial classification tasks, and zero unified visibility into distribution drift.

1. Four Pillars of a Production Enterprise AI Gateway

  • Adaptive Concurrency & Priority Shedding: Static rate limits either starve bursty interactive copilots or overload upstream token quotas during batch jobs. Borrowing TCP-Vegas / Gradient2 queue-delay control alongside dual token-bucket priority tiers keeps interactive user traffic responsive while throttling background batch document runs gracefully.
  • Speculative Cascade Routing: Route well-scoped extraction, classification, and formatting requests to fast, cost-efficient tiers first, escalating to frontier reasoning models (such as GPT-5, Claude Opus/Sonnet, or Gemini Pro) only when confidence or structural verifiers signal ambiguity.
  • Inline Claim-Faithfulness Guardrails: Enforce lightweight atomic grounding checks on RAG-backed responses before returning payloads to downstream applications.
  • PSI & Jensen-Shannon Statistical Drift Plane: Monitor Population Stability Index (PSI) and base-2 Jensen-Shannon divergence across prompt embeddings, token lengths, and refusal rates so platform teams detect upstream data shifts before business KPIs degrade.
