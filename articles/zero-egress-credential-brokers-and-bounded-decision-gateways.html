Out-of-Sandbox Credential Brokers & Bounded Decision Gateways: Default-Deny Egress, Speaker Scopes & Sliced Prefill

Published 2026-11-28 · Nagarjuna Kamireddy

Once an enterprise agent ingests external emails, tickets, or repository issues, its context window must be treated as untrusted compute. At the same time, routing every intermediate triage or ranking step through open-ended generative decoding inflates tail latency and schema failure rates. Two deterministic control planes resolve these security and latency bottlenecks outside the prompt:

  • Prompt-Injected Secret Leaks & Monitor Fragility (arXiv:2611.34934): Stripping surface formatting cues caused a retired production monitor's false-negative rate to jump 46x (from 0.5% to 23.0%), underscoring why agents should never hold raw API keys in container memory.
  • Multi-Speaker Authority Bleed (arXiv:2611.34920): In shared multi-user threads, open-weight agents drop below 20% joint task success when unprivileged participants nudge privileged tool calls.
  • Generative Latency & 64K Prefill Stalls (arXiv:2611.34615): Paying prose-generation latency on closed-set 1-of-N decisions and running unchunked 64K prefills breaks interactive <2s TTFT SLOs.

1. External Credential Substitution & Speaker Scopes (aegis-runtime v1.2.0)

In aegis-runtime (src/aegis/policy/credential_broker.py), OpaqueSecretBroker injects only HMAC-tagged sec_ref:// handles into container environment variables. An external default-deny HTTPS supervisor blocks raw, Base64, hex, and URL-escaped secret exfiltration, substitutes real tokens exclusively on allowlisted (actor_id, host, method, path_prefix) routes, and emits canonical PolicyDiffProposal digests on blocked routes without restarting pods. Alongside it, MultiActorCapabilitySession binds every group-thread tool call to the invoking speaker's verified capability set.

2. Bounded Zero-Text Decision Routing & Near-Tie Guards (prism-gate v1.1.0)

Production studies show replacing generative prose on closed-set 1-of-N decisions lifts <2s SLO attainment from ~90% to 99.2% while eliminating invalid schema outputs. prism-gate (src/prism_gate/routing/bounded_decision_router.py) classifies closed-set options via calibrated softmax margins and normalized Shannon entropy, flags logit gaps smaller than 2^-6 vulnerable to 18.0% cross-accelerator bfloat16 rank flips (arXiv:2611.34462), and escalates ambiguous inputs to iterative reasoning tiers (arXiv:2611.34923).

3. Latency-Slack Prefill Slicing, Switch-Adapted Drift & Signed Receipts

To prevent 64K-token document prefills from head-of-line blocking short interactive requests, compute_pipeaxes_slice_plan sizes prefill micro-slices against active TTFT slack budgets with 3.75x Hadamard-INT4 transport compression, paired with SwitchAdaptedDriftTracker (arXiv:2611.34905), HMAC-SHA256 stream-verified SignedBillingReceipt tokens (arXiv:2611.34623), and 90%+ episodic log compression in company-os v1.1.0.
