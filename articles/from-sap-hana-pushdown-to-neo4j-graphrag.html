From 48-Hour Batch Loads to 12-Minute SAP HANA Pushdown & 6-Agent Neo4j GraphRAG

Published November 16, 2026 · 4× SAP Certified · AWS Solutions Architect Pro · NVIDIA Certified Agentic AI

1. Lesson One From SAP HANA: Push Compute to Structure

Long before LLMs entered the enterprise, I spent twelve years embedded at client sites across the UK, Malaysia, Singapore, the US, and India building SAP BW, BPC, BusinessObjects, and SAP HANA architectures. At PETRONAS Energy Trading & Deloitte in Kuala Lumpur (2011–2012), moving analytical logic from disk-bound application loops into in-memory columnar HANA models cut a critical data load from 48 hours to 12 minutes. At GlaxoSmithKline (GSK) in Brentford, UK (2014–2016), running regression, random forest, SVM, clustering, and ARIMA/Holt-Winters forecasting directly inside SAP HANA's Predictive Analysis Library (PAL, APL, and embedded R) compressed MRP and financial consolidation batch windows from hours to minutes — and sparked my transition into full-time Machine Learning.

2. Why Naive Flat Chunking Breaks Enterprise RAG

Today, many Generative AI teams repeat the exact mistake early BI teams made: they strip rich structural hierarchy out of enterprise documents by slicing PDFs into naive 500-token flat vector chunks. When an assembly manual or SAP financial hierarchy loses parent-child relationships, part dependencies, and applicability scopes, vector similarity retrieves plausible-sounding text from the wrong subsystem.

3. Structure-Preserving Ingestion + 6-Agent Neo4j GraphRAG

At John Deere, we solved this by preserving document structure end-to-end during ingestion and backing a 6-agent authoring team with a Neo4j knowledge graph. Each specialized agent traverses explicit entity and section relationships alongside hybrid search so every generated paragraph cites its authoritative source node. Paired with standardized OAuth 2.0 Model Context Protocol (MCP) adapters for enterprise systems, structure-first architecture turns 20 years of ERP/PLM discipline into trustworthy agentic AI.
